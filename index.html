Mission 03 · Tunis, summer 2026. Talan Tunisia, Innovation Factory. GenAI engineer intern.

The project: Urban Mind, a generative AI platform to plan the city of tomorrow.

A squad of 13. Her post: the AI that answers the planners.

Then it rose over the city: The Hallucination. It never doubts. It is often wrong.

Her weapon: the law itself. 9,000 articles, every one indexed.

Hybrid search: keywords and meaning cross on the one article that answers.

The LLM proposes, the code computes. Every figure is checked before it is shown.

4 agent pipelines. At every gate, a human validates.

Every answer must cite its source.
