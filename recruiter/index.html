Recrutia

PFA · team of 3

A chat-first recruitment platform: it understands who you are, candidate or recruiter, and runs the right agents.

  • 7 specialised agents for candidates: job offers scraped from 4 sources (LinkedIn, RemoteOK, WeWorkRemotely, Adzuna), a CV–job match score, advice on the CV.
  • For recruiters: CV scoring, LinkedIn job posts, invitation and rejection emails, all written by the agents.
  • Safe by design: the OWASP LLM Top 10 checks and a gateway that anonymises the personal data in CVs.

Agentic RAG Chatbot

PFA ISAMM 2025–26 · team of 2

An educational chatbot that plans its search, reads the course or the web, and checks its own answers.

  • Breaks a hard question into parallel sub-tasks and routes each one to the course PDFs or to the web.
  • A grader corrects its own answers (CRAG) before it replies; a long-term memory remembers the student.
  • Summaries on the fly cut ~90% of the tokens; two models share the work for decisions under 500 ms.
Team
Oumaima Jouini, Ines Cherif · supervised by Dr. Oualid Khayati
Stack
LangGraph · LangChain · Groq (Llama 3.1 · 3.3) · ChromaDB · Tavily · FastAPI · React

SurgiGuard AI

HackCure hackathon · team EvolTech (6)

It watches over patients recovering from surgery at home, and alerts the care team in time.

  • A wearable (ESP32: heart rate, SpO₂, motion, temperature) streams the vitals over MQTT.
  • A multi-agent AI (LangGraph) spots a change in the vitals, investigates it and alerts the care team.
  • Medical documents become a personal care plan with a RAG assistant; computer vision checks wound photos and rehab exercises.
Team
Ines Cherif, Med Aziz Hraghi, Karoui Mohamed Fares, Elaa Elfahem, Yosr Garma, Wassila Ben Slimene
Stack
ESP32 · MQTT · .NET 8 · MongoDB · FastAPI · LangGraph · React Native · Kotlin
